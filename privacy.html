Seshat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Seshat is a personal, single-user, self-hosted application. It collects no data from third parties and serves no other users.

What is stored: TikTok OAuth tokens (to publish on the operator's own account), the operator's own scheduled and published content, and standard application logs — all stored locally on hardware owned and controlled by the operator.

What is never done: no third-party data collection, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no data sharing or sale, no AI training on collected data.

TikTok data: used only to authenticate the operator's account and publish the operator's own videos (video.upload draft flow). If content is deleted by the operator, it is deleted from Seshat's storage as well.

Contact: kgdaghost@gmail.com — requests about stored data can be made at any time.
